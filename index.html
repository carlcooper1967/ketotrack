Understanding Your Loan Options
▼
Standard loan not backed by the government. Best rates go to borrowers with strong credit and 20% down. With less than 20% down PMI is required until you reach 20% equity. Available in 10, 15, 20, and 30-year terms.
▼
Government-backed loan insured by the Federal Housing Administration. As low as 3.5% down with a 580+ credit score. More flexible qualifying requirements make this a popular choice for first-time buyers. Requires both upfront and monthly mortgage insurance (MIP).
▼
Exclusively for eligible veterans, active-duty service members, and surviving spouses. Zero down payment required, no monthly mortgage insurance, and highly competitive rates. A one-time VA Funding Fee applies. One of the best loan products available - if you qualify, use it.
▼
100% financing for eligible rural and suburban properties - zero down payment required. More North Texas properties qualify than most people realize including many in Parker, Hood, Wise, and Johnson counties. Income limits apply. Ask your lender if your target area qualifies.
▼
For loan amounts above the conventional conforming limit (currently $766,550 in most Texas counties). Typically requires stronger credit, larger down payment (10-20%), and more cash reserves. Rates are competitive but qualification standards are stricter.
▼
This is the one most buyers never hear about. DPA programs are available through Texas state, county, and city agencies and can cover part or all of your down payment and sometimes closing costs too. Income and purchase price limits apply but many buyers are surprised to find they qualify. Ask your lender specifically about DPA programs available in your area - it could be the difference between renting and owning.
